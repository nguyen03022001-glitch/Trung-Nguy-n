Our story

Lunara is a study in restraint: luminous pearls, sculptural forms, and the beauty of keeping what matters close.

A softer standard

Jewelry with a sense of place.

Born between the sea and the city, Lunara Pearl Co. brings a quiet, modern sensibility to one of nature’s most enduring treasures. We work with freshwater, Akoya, and South Sea pearls—choosing each for its luster, shape, and singular point of view.

Our designs are intentionally versatile. A line of light at the collarbone. A small, luminous punctuation. Pieces that don’t ask for attention, but always reward it.

With intention, always.

Our philosophy
